# Valuation and company research
Original educational note for AdvisorIQ, not current Apple analysis.
Valuation research compares market price with business performance, cash flows, earnings assumptions and uncertainty. A price-to-earnings ratio alone cannot establish that a stock is cheap. Compare growth assumptions, margins, debt, cash generation and peer differences. Read the latest issuer filings and their dates before making claims about Apple or any other company. Past price changes do not establish future returns. This sample library contains no current financial statements or forward-looking valuation model.
Further reading: SEC EDGAR — https://www.sec.gov/edgar/search/
