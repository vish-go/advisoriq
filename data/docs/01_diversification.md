# Diversification and concentration risk
Original educational note for AdvisorIQ, not a company-specific research report.
A concentrated portfolio depends heavily on a small number of holdings. Diversification across companies, sectors and asset classes can reduce exposure to a single issuer, but does not eliminate market losses. Before adding Apple or another stock, examine its share of the existing portfolio and overlap with funds already held. Sector labels alone do not describe every shared risk. This note contains no current price target or buy recommendation.
